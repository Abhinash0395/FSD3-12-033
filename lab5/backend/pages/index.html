Good questions welcome

Things you might want to know.

Here are a few useful details to consider before starting online coaching.

How do online classes work?
Online classes typically take place through a video meeting platform. The teacher explains concepts, answers questions, and guides practice. The exact platform and schedule should be confirmed when booking.
Can I choose the subject I need help with?
Yes. Use the enquiry form to tell us which subject, grade, or skill you want to work on. The team can then discuss suitable learning options and current availability.
Do I need to be an advanced learner?
No. You can describe your current level and the topics you find difficult. A learning plan can start with foundational concepts and progress at a comfortable pace.
How much do classes cost?
Fees depend on the subject, lesson frequency, session format, and learning goals. Submit an enquiry to request the current fee structure before making a payment.
How do I book a free class?
Complete the enquiry form below with your contact details and preferred subject. The form on this demo page displays a confirmation locally; it does not send information to an academy until a backend or form service is connected.
